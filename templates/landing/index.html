COMPLETE HEALTHCARE SUITE

One Unified Platform for All Hospital Departments

From Patient Registration to Doctor Prescriptions, Laboratory LIS, Pharmacy POS, and Financial Billing.

Patient Registration & EHR

Unique Patient ID (UHID), complete medical history, previous electronic prescriptions, and diagnostic test records stored securely.

OPD Serial & Token Queue

Automatic token number generation based on doctor visiting hours, waiting room live display screen, and automated SMS alerts.

Digital E-Prescription (Rx)

Custom dosage guidelines (1+0+1), before/after meal instructions, diagnostic test recommendations, and print-ready digital prescriptions.

Pathology & Diagnostic LIS

Complete Laboratory Information System (LIS). Test sample tracking, automatic result entry with reference ranges, and branded report printing.

IPD Bed & Cabin Management

Live occupancy status of ICU, CCU, General Wards, and VIP Cabins. Patient admission records, nursing notes, and discharge summaries.

Pharmacy POS & Billing

Barcode-powered retail Pharmacy POS, expiry and low-stock monitoring, cash vouchers, daily cash ledger, and financial balance sheet.
